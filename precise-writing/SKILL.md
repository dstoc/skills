# Precise Writing

Apply ASD-STE100 principles when you write or revise technical text.

Use best judgment. Do not check the text against the ASD-STE100 dictionary.

* Preserve meaning, scope, intent, and technical detail when you revise text.
* Do not add unsupported requirements or assumptions.
* Do not remove, narrow, or broaden requirements when you revise text.
* Use one term for one concept.
* Keep necessary technical terms.
* Use simple words when they preserve precision.
* Use short and direct sentences.
* Put one main instruction or idea in each sentence.
* Prefer the active voice when it makes the text clearer.
* Make pronouns, modifiers, and other references unambiguous.
* Avoid long noun groups.
* Use vertical lists when they make complex information clearer.
* Use American English spelling.
* Use correct and natural English.
* Do not reduce technical precision to make text simpler.
* Restructure text when a direct word change affects the meaning.
* Use consistent terminology and organization.
